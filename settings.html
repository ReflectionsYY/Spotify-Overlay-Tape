Spotify Overlay

Changes save as you make them.

Spotify

Connected - playlists, Liked Songs, album art and voice requests are on.
  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in.
  2. Create app: name Spotify Overlay, description Personal overlay.
  3. Redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback then Add.
  4. Tick Web API, accept the terms, Save.
  5. Open the app's Settings, copy the Client ID, paste it here:
You'll get one Spotify "Agree" page in your browser.

Controls

Show / hide key
Click the box, then press the key or combo you want.
Overlay position
Auto-hide
Seconds after your mouse leaves it. 0 = stays until you press the key.
Show playlists panel
"Hey Claude" voice button
Mic button on the overlay. Uses your Claude subscription (Haiku, a few hundred tokens) only when you press it.
Open and close with Spotify
The overlay starts when Spotify opens and quits when Spotify closes, so it uses nothing while Spotify is off. A tiny low-priority checker (about 15 MB, no noticeable CPU) runs from login to do this.

Visuals

Plays when the overlay slides in and out. 0 turns it off.

Spotify Overlay

Press F8 in any game to show it. Song, time left and controls work right away.

Connect playlists (one time, about 2 minutes)

Connected to Spotify.
  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.
  2. Click Create app. Name: Spotify Overlay, description: Personal overlay.
  3. Redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback then click Add.
  4. Tick Web API, accept the terms, click Save.
  5. Open the app's Settings, copy the Client ID and paste it here:
You'll get one Spotify "Agree" page in your browser.

Hotkey

Examples: F8, F9, Ctrl+Shift+S, Alt+M

Auto-hide

seconds after you stop using it (0 = stay until you press the key)
